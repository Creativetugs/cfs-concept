CORVALLIS FOLKLORE SOCIETY — ONE-PAGE WEBSITE CONCEPT

Files:
- index.html: page structure and draft content
- styles.css: responsive visual design
- script.js: mobile navigation, current year, and demo membership form notice
- assets/cfs-logo.png: logo supplied with the brief

Preview:
Open index.html in a browser. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.

Important handoff notes:
- This is a front-end concept, not connected to WordPress, MemberPlanet, a calendar feed, a newsletter platform, a payment processor, or a donation system.
- Event details and times were taken from the supplied brief and should be verified by CFS before publication.
- The current membership amount ($10 per year per email address) is shown with a note to confirm current dues because the supplied membership information is dated.
- The donation button opens an email inquiry because no verified donation URL was supplied.
- The membership form is a visual/demo form and does not transmit or store personal information.
- Some events in the supplied content may have passed by the time the page is published; update the calendar before launch.

PHOTO / INTEGRATION NOTES
- The concept uses remotely hosted Unsplash photos. Replace them with CFS-owned event photographs before production where possible, and confirm image licensing/attribution requirements for selected assets.
- A slim Creative Tugs bar sits at the very top with clickable links to https://creativetugs.com/ and https://calendly.com/creativetugs/30min.
- Calendar, MemberPlanet, newsletter, donations/payment processing, and member login still require real service integration and testing.

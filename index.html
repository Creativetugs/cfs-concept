A long-running local tradition

Our story is still being sung.

For decades, CFS volunteers have helped bring performers, dancers, singers and neighbors together around folk arts.

c. 1974

The beginnings

A folk community takes root

CFS history traces the society’s beginnings to the mid-1970s, when local folk enthusiasts began building a network for music, dance and shared cultural expression.

1977

Official gatherings

First newsletter and dances

The first CFS newsletter appeared in May 1977, and the first recorded CFS square dance followed that June at Unitarian Fellowship.

1979–1980

More ways to gather

Song circles and the Best Cellar

Song circles became a continuing tradition, and the Best Cellar Coffeehouse was revived as part of CFS in February 1980. The first Oregon Folklife Festival took place that year.

2005

A new dance tradition

Contra Dance Weekend begins

The first Corvallis Contra Dance Weekend was held in February 2005, growing the society’s tradition of welcoming social dance.

2013

Growing together

More cultures in the circle

Hoolyeh International Folkdancers became part of CFS, adding another way for the community to explore dance traditions from around the world.

Today

The next verse

The story needs your voice

Concerts, dances, open stages, festivals and song circles continue because people show up, volunteer, participate and invite others in.
